Machine Learning · Computer Vision

D-FuseSmileNet · Enhancing Spontaneous Smile Recognition

A novel deep learning framework that fuses transformer embeddings with hand-crafted physiological D-marker features using Hadamard interactions—simplifying multi-task complexity while pushing state-of-the-art accuracy on smile authenticity benchmarks.

Technical Challenge & Innovation

  • Problem: Genuine smiles show subtle spatiotemporal cues (orbicularis oculi activation, symmetry) often lost in standard deep nets.
  • Hadamard Fusion: Direct multiplicative fusion between transformer features and D-marker descriptors enabled expressive interactions without auxiliary loss weighting.
  • Architecture: Benchmarked 15 fusion strategies; Hadamard fusion delivered peak accuracy while avoiding manual landmark initialization.
  • Automation: Fully trainable end-to-end pipeline surpassing complex multi-task learning baselines.

Architecture Diagram

100% Accuracy

Achieved perfect classification on the challenging BBC dataset, and 99.7% on MMI, establishing consistent SOTA performance.

+5.0% Lift

Outperformed previous multi-task learning methods by significant margins: +5.0% on BBC and +0.8% on UvA-NEMO, validating the superiority of direct feature interaction.

Simplified Training

Eliminated the need for auxiliary loss-weighting and hyperparameter tuning required by Multi-Task Learning (MTL) approaches, improving computational efficiency.

Natural Language Processing · LLMs

LLM-Driven Clinical NER Annotation Pipeline

Designed a scalable annotation loop harnessing GPT-4.1 with Chain-of-Thought prompts to label low-resource Bengali clinical text, dramatically narrowing the performance gap to fully supervised models while eliminating manual labeling cost.

Core Methodology & Impact

  • Challenge: Scarcity of human-annotated Bengali medical corpora limits clinical NER quality.
  • CoT Prompting: Structured reasoning prompts outperformed raw capacity—GPT-4.1-mini + CoT exceeded GPT-4.1 ICL.
  • Corpus Insights: Deep dive on Bangla-HealthNER guided entity-specific prompt strategies for noisy code-switched text.

Annotation Pipeline Diagram

3× F1 Lift

CoT reasoning almost tripled token-level F1 agreement with human labels (from 0.120 to 0.335) compared to plain prompting.

46.0 Macro F1

Closed two-thirds of the gap to full supervision without human labels. CoT reasoning almost tripled token-level F1 agreement with human labels (from 0.120 to 0.335) compared to plain prompting.
